# Research areas

`geta-pfmp-research-envelope.geojson` is a provisional, coarse bounding box
derived from the location range printed in the Kenya Forest Service Geta
Participatory Forest Management Plan (2026–2031). It exists so source coverage
research and engineering dry-runs can proceed while an authoritative GIS
boundary and reuse terms are pending.

The rectangle is not the Geta Forest Station boundary. It includes surrounding
land, assumes WGS84 because the source text does not state a geographic datum,
and interprets the plan's quote-like coordinate marks as minutes. It must not be
used for operational alert filtering, forest-area calculations,
legal scope, public incident claims, or enforcement decisions. Keep every
result produced with it labelled provisional. Replace it with a verified,
lawfully reusable KFS geometry when available; retain the source/version and
record the transformation.

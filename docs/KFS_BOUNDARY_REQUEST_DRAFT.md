**Subject:** Request for official Geta Forest Station GIS boundary and data-use conditions

Dear Kenya Forest Service Survey and Mapping team,

I am preparing a small, owner-operated research pilot for ForestProof, an evidence and response workflow for forest monitoring. The current area reference is the Geta Participatory Forest Management Plan (2026–2031), which describes Geta Forest Station as the whole Kipipiri Forest Reserve plus part of Aberdares Forest.

Could you please advise whether KFS can provide or point me to:

1. The authoritative machine-readable boundary for Geta Forest Station, Kipipiri Forest Reserve, and the relevant Aberdares Forest portion, preferably as GeoPackage, GeoJSON, or shapefile, with the coordinate reference system, datum, version/date, and source citation.
2. A public download or service (such as WMS/WFS or another API), if one exists, and the authoritative method for deriving the Geta Station boundary from its component reserves.
3. The terms for displaying the boundary in a private pilot, creating a simplified derived geometry for an area-of-interest query, and later using it in a commercial service, including required attribution or restrictions on redistribution.
4. Confirmation of how the supplied geometry corresponds to the 21,644.37 ha stated in the Geta PFMP and its January 2025 map.
5. Any fees or other costs for obtaining or using the boundary, including API/service charges or commercial-use licensing fees. Please provide any cost details before we decide whether to proceed.

The pilot would use the boundary only to scope and filter upstream satellite observations for human review. It would not treat an alert as proof of illegal activity or publish sensitive case locations.

Thank you for your guidance.

This is an information request only and does not authorize any paid service, license, or purchase.

# **ForestProof: independent product and design assessment**

**What you want is achievable on your current infrastructure.** You do not need an expensive mapping subscription, a dedicated GPU server or a complete rewrite to deliver a beautiful, immersive, geographically grounded conservation product.

Having examined the deployed site, I now understand the disconnect between what you envisioned and what Codex delivered.

Codex has built a reasonably coherent scientific reporting website. What you envisioned is closer to an **interactive planetary observatory combined with a scientific evidence laboratory**.

Those are fundamentally different experiences.

I've also recovered our earlier **Living Atlas** design concept from November 19: an atmospheric Earth, emerald and cyan geography, geological relief, a descent from planetary scale into a local observation, and a connected visual investigation. That remains an excellent foundation.

The biggest problem is not the colour scheme, typography or even the absence of animation.

**It is the absence of spatial continuity.**

ForestProof is fundamentally about places, observations of those places over time, and decisions about what happened there. Yet the current experience rarely allows you to feel that you are investigating an actual place.

That should become the organizing principle of the redesigned product.

# **7. The Golden Economics and Still-Fulcrum opportunity**

ForestProof should not attempt to own satellites, launch spacecraft or replicate every scientific provider.

Its potential economic position is downstream of observation but upstream of consequential decisions.

The beautiful public atlas is the acquisition and engagement layer.

The observation-to-outcome workflow is the potential economic fulcrum.

The authorized, trustworthy accumulation of reviewed observations, decisions and verified outcomes is the potential supermoat.

Those commercial advantages must eventually be validated with real conservation practitioners. Visual delight can attract people, but they will only continue using ForestProof if it helps them accomplish meaningful work.

I would give Codex the following directive. It explicitly defines what you mean by an immersive visual identity so that it does not interpret the request as merely changing colours, adding gradients or placing satellite images inside dashboard cards.

# FORESTPROOF — LIVING ATLAS 2.0

## Master product transformation directive

Transform the existing ForestProof product into an immersive planetary observatory, geographic exploration environment and scientific evidence laboratory.

This is an evolution of the existing repository and Contabo deployment, not a new application.

The goal is to achieve exceptional visual delight, geographic immersion, scientific credibility and practical coherence simultaneously.

## 1. Product identity

ForestProof helps people understand environmental observations and follow their evidence into responsible human decisions.

The canonical workflow is:

Observation → Provenance → Human review → Recorded response → Dated follow-up → Reproducible dossier.

The central visual metaphor is progressive descent:

Planet → Country → Landscape → Observation → Evidence → Decision.

Do not treat ForestProof as a generic administrative dashboard, landing-page template or collection of disconnected cards.

## 2. Primary audiences

**Public visitors and conservation enthusiasts:** Understand a landscape, explore actual satellite observations and learn how environmental evidence is interpreted.

**Conservation practitioners:** Examine trustworthy sources, retain uncertainty, own investigations and document responses.

**Researchers and technical contributors:** Inspect methodologies, reproduce experiments and understand source limitations.

**Grant evaluators:** Independently distinguish implemented capabilities, experimental results, operational limitations and proposed future research.

Prioritize the public discovery experience immediately. Preserve the operational workflow and advance its qualification independently.

## 3. Architecture audit

Before modifying implementation:

- Read AGENTS.md, DESIGN.md, PROJECT_BRAIN.md, execution/CURRENT_STATUS.md, the project map and launch requirements.
- Inspect the existing public templates and rendering tools under tools/sentinel_visibility.
- Inspect packages/ui/living-atlas.css.
- Inspect the existing MapLibre components in apps/web.
- Locate the archived Earth artwork, source imagery manifests and geographic research geometry.
- Record the current production commits, test baseline, deployment controls and resource usage.
- Preserve existing real data, private records, scheduled jobs and working public routes.

Avoid introducing a parallel framework or database. Extend existing foundations.

## 4. Visual design system

Implement Living Atlas 2.0 using:

- Orbital black: #071511
- Deep forest: #102B23
- Terrain slate: #203B34
- Bioluminescent mint: #8CE4BD
- Scientific cyan: #65D5E5
- Evidence ivory: #F2F0E5
- Uncertainty amber: #E8B86A

Retain elegant editorial typography and technical monospace metadata.

Use depth through geographically meaningful terrain, layered evidence, atmospheric perspective and restrained motion.

Use one principal visual focal point per screen. Avoid gratuitous glassmorphism, glowing charts, excessive cards, decorative grid clutter and meaningless particle effects.

Mint represents navigation and primary actions. Cyan represents observations. Amber represents uncertainty. Red is reserved for actual errors or explicitly defined findings.

## 5. Public homepage — planetary entrance

Route: /welcome

Create a visually dominant Earth scene displaying the geographic relationship between Earth, Kenya and the provisional Geta research area.

Use the existing Earth artwork if suitable and appropriately licensed. A lightweight static render is sufficient initially. An optional interactive globe may follow.

Required features:

- Atmospheric Earth composition.
- Geographically accurate Kenya locator.
- Clear explanation of ForestProof.
- Primary action: Explore Geta.
- Secondary action: Follow an investigation.
- Explicit distinction between real public observations and fictional demonstrations.
- Existing two-date Sentinel-2 teaching example immediately accessible.

The Earth visual must not imply global operational monitoring or a current satellite feed.

Acceptance criteria:

- A new visitor can explain what ForestProof does within 30 seconds.
- Geta is identifiable as a research area in Kenya.
- Navigation works without WebGL or JavaScript.
- The existing historical image data and interpretations remain accurate.
- Mobile visitors are not forced through a long cinematic animation.

## 6. Public atlas — geographic exploration

Proposed new route: /atlas/geta

Retain /visibility as the functioning observation report and deep-link destination.

Extend the existing MapLibre approach to create a public geographic observatory.

Required interactions:

- Pan, zoom, rotate and reset view.
- Toggle between 2D and optional 3D terrain.
- Locate the verified provisional research rectangle.
- Select observation date and grid.
- Display acquisition date and source identity.
- Inspect source-image availability.
- Open the observation laboratory at the selected grid/date.

Design desktop as a geographic viewport with collapsible controls and an observation inspector.

Design mobile as a geographic viewport with a compact date control and an accessible bottom evidence sheet.

Terrain must originate from a documented, legally usable elevation dataset. The research rectangle must originate from the existing canonical geometry.

If terrain has not been qualified, ship a documented 2D geographic experience first.

Do not invent geographic coverage cells, disturbance polygons or alert locations.

Acceptance criteria:

- The displayed rectangle matches the canonical research geometry.
- Its provisional status is continuously clear.
- Camera actions do not modify scientific measurements.
- Map selection and date selection stay synchronized.
- Terrain-source failures provide a functional 2D fallback.
- No global tile archive or expensive recurring map subscription is introduced without approval.

## 7. Observation laboratory

Route: /visibility

Transform the existing report into a spatially coherent investigation instrument.

Retain all existing date/grid combinations, exact source-derived values and no-script reading.

Add:

- Larger source-image viewport.
- Side-by-side comparison for compatible available dates.
- Image magnification.
- Reliable observation-selection state.
- Expandable source receipt.
- Clear source-quality and interpretation panels.
- Shareable grid/date URLs.
- Optional geographic quality layers only when verified spatial rasters exist and publication is authorized.

Preserve the distinction between:

- Observation acquisition date.
- Source verification time.
- Publication time.
- Image context.
- Aggregate classification coverage.
- Actual geographic quality distributions.

Only T36MZE on 8 and 13 November 2026 currently has matching published imagery.

Other selections must explicitly state that matching image context is unavailable.

Do not reuse a different date's image to fill a missing state.

Acceptance criteria:

- Every published date/grid pair reproduces its verified counts.
- Coverage calculations preserve their correct denominators.
- Overlapping grids are never incorrectly summed.
- Images are matched by explicit date/grid identifiers.
- The 8 November T36MZE result remains 6,424 visible centres out of 27,722, including 6,346 classified as vegetation.
- The 13 November T36MZE result remains three visible centres out of 27,722.
- Missing imagery never prevents access to available aggregate evidence.
- No view equates unassessed ground with unchanged forest.

## 8. Evidence walkthrough

Route: /tour

Preserve the existing browser-only fictional simulation and build a more immersive investigation chamber around it.

Provide:

- A persistent evidence viewport.
- Five clearly identified workflow stages.
- A growing case lineage graph.
- Review choices with meaningful consequences.
- A documented next action and follow-up.
- Inspectable completed dossier records.

The same fictional source identity must persist across the entire investigation.

Do not silently import real Geta coverage into a fictional disturbance investigation.

Acceptance criteria:

- Both existing review branches remain functional.
- Editing an earlier decision resets affected downstream steps.
- No public walkthrough state is written to the private backend.
- The fictional status remains visible throughout.
- JSON and print/PDF exports remain explicitly fictional and unsigned.
- No response is presented as having contacted an external person or system.

## 9. Forest Intervention Outcome Graph

Build a reusable evidence-lineage visualization driven by existing case records.

Node types:

- Observation
- Original source receipt
- Case
- Review decision
- Response
- Follow-up
- Supporting evidence
- Dossier

Edges must represent real recorded relationships, not decorative connections.

Each node inspector should display applicable identifiers, timestamps, source references, actor information, uncertainty and related records.

Use lightweight SVG or an appropriate accessible graph component. Do not make this feature dependent on a graph database or 3D engine.

The public graph uses the fictional walkthrough state. The protected graph uses authorized saved records.

Acceptance criteria:

- Missing relationships have explicit unavailable states.
- Private details cannot leak into public projections.
- Graph updates correspond exactly to recorded workflow changes.
- A conventional accessible timeline presents the same information.

## 12. Protected application

Extend the same visual identity into the existing authorized workspace without changing its security boundaries.

## 13. Data and provenance contracts

Extend existing manifests where necessary to support presentation.

Each published imagery asset should have its source, date, grid, spatial reference, geographic bounds, resolution, licence or attribution, availability and integrity reference.

Each terrain asset should have its dataset identity, resolution, geographic bounds, processing method, attribution and publication terms.

Every displayed record must belong to an explicit category:

- Real source-derived observation
- Historical research
- Illustrative contextual artwork
- Fictional walkthrough
- Synthetic protected DEMO data
- Authorized real case

Never allow presentation styling to blur these categories.

Missing, stale, unavailable, loading and failed states must be explicitly implemented.

## 14. Performance and infrastructure

Use the existing Contabo deployment.

Prefer:

- Static, immutable assets.
- Locally cached regional map tiles.
- PMTiles or another appropriately scoped static tile format.
- Client-side WebGL.
- Deferred loading of heavy geographic components.
- SVG/CSS for diagrams and evidence instruments.
- Offline raster and terrain preparation.
- Existing source-refresh scheduling.

Avoid mandatory external requests on the principal public journey.

Initial engineering budgets, subject to measurement:

- Public landing-page transfer: target under 1.5 MB.
- Optional initial geographic experience: target under 5 MB transferred.
- Regional geographic asset storage: initially cap at 500 MB pending review.
- Incremental recurring subscriptions: $0 target.

Measure actual resource usage before release. Document any budget exceptions.

Support reduced motion, no-WebGL operation, keyboard access and mobile touch.

Never request or expose credentials, private evidence, API secrets or database files.

## 15. Operational launch — separate workstream

Visual completion does not satisfy operational readiness.

Before enabling a live alert service:

1. Qualify a source and approved operating area together.
2. Verify repeated useful observations, access costs and publication rights.
3. Establish production owner identity.
4. Complete a genuine observation-to-persistent-case workflow.
5. Recover the original receipt, case and dossier from an independently protected copy.
6. Verify authorized publication rules and operational failure handling.

A real historical observation and a separately stored review do not establish that this complete workflow exists.

Keep operational claims gated until the necessary evidence has been independently verified.

## 16. Grant credibility

Create a concise public capability register that distinguishes:

- Implemented and accessible.
- Implemented but synthetic.
- Historical research evidence.
- Experimentally investigated.
- Not yet qualified.
- Proposed with grant funding.

Include a reproducibility index, dated technical milestones and research limitations.

Do not imply verified ecological impact, established customer traction, production monitoring or a validated competitive moat without evidence.

## 17. Development order

Phase 0: Audit the existing implementation, baseline performance and create desktop/mobile visual compositions.

Phase 1: Deliver the planetary homepage and shared visual/navigation system.

Phase 2: Deliver the geographic atlas and integrate the existing observation laboratory.

Phase 3: Deliver the interactive evidence lineage and improved walkthrough.

Phase 4: Extend the research laboratory, guide and protected workspace identity.

Phase 5: Conduct comprehensive regression, accessibility, performance and scientific-integrity testing.

Work on operational qualification as a separate explicitly tracked sequence.

After each phase, provide actual screenshots and test results rather than merely describing the intended appearance.

## 18. Release acceptance

Exercise desktop and mobile viewport widths of 1440, 390 and 320 pixels.

Test navigation, date/grid selection, URL persistence, missing imagery, blocked tiles, WebGL failure, no-script behavior, reduced motion, keyboard access, fictional exports, private-route protection and existing saved-case functionality.

Run existing CI suites and add regression tests for new features.

Document the exact deployment commit, measured resource usage, source limitations and rollback procedure.

Preserve current gateway configuration, timers, private data and unrelated VPS services.

Do not promote a failed or scientifically misleading implementation merely because its screenshots are attractive.

## Final design principle

Make the visual experience extraordinary because it reveals the structure of reality more clearly.

Not because it invents a more impressive reality than the available evidence supports.

They provide:

- Satellite imagery
- Radar observations
- Fire alerts
- Forest-cover change
- Vegetation indices
- Historical environmental data
- Predictive land-use analysis

An alert must still be:

3. Evaluated against legitimate environmental and land-management context.
4. Reviewed for credibility.
5. Assigned to an appropriate investigator.
6. Verified or rejected.
7. Connected to action.
8. Followed up.
9. Measured for effectiveness.

FORESTPROOF owns the workflow connecting these stages.

**"Turn environmental disturbances into evidence-backed investigation cases, track the authorized response, and measure what happened afterward."**

Our business promise:

**"Help organizations responsible for protecting environmental assets identify, investigate and document threats using less manual effort and better evidence."**

We do not replace:

- Satellite operators
- Forest rangers
- Environmental scientists
- Courts
- Kenya Forest Service
- Environmental regulators
- Licensed environmental professionals

# 7. LEGAL AND EVIDENCE DISCIPLINE

The project is motivated by public reporting about forest cutting, harvesting and legal disputes in the Aberdares.

Recent reporting describes an October 2026 court intervention concerning logging.

Do NOT hardcode reported court orders as permanently applicable law.

Before displaying legal status:

- Find the official order or reliable legal record where available.
- Record the issuing court.
- Record case identification.
- Record decision date.
- Record operative provisions.
- Record effective dates.
- Record geographic scope.
- Record any limitations.
- Record later amendments or stays.
- Mark whether a qualified human has verified the source.

A press article is not equivalent to the official order.

Never automatically label an observation:

"ILLEGAL LOGGING"

solely because tree cover declined.

Instead, distinguish:

- FOREST_DISTURBANCE_OBSERVED
- POSSIBLE_HARVESTING
- POSSIBLE_FIRE_DAMAGE
- POSSIBLE_STORM_DAMAGE
- PLANTATION_MANAGEMENT_POSSIBLE
- AUTHORIZATION_UNKNOWN
- DOCUMENTED_AUTHORIZATION
- POTENTIAL_POLICY_CONFLICT
- VERIFIED_RESTRICTION_APPLIES
- REQUIRES_HUMAN_REVIEW
- VERIFIED_INCIDENT
- FALSE_POSITIVE
- INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE

A legally consequential classification requires appropriate evidence and human review.

FORESTPROOF is decision support, not a court or enforcement agency.

# 12. OBSERVATION MODEL

An environmental observation is not yet an incident.

Create a typed observation contract.

Conceptually:

```
type EnvironmentalObservation = {
  id: string;
  sourceId: string;

  sourceProduct: string;
  sourceRecordId: string;

  observedAt: string;
  publishedAt?: string;
  ingestedAt: string;

  geometry: GeoJSON.Geometry;

  observationType:
    | "FOREST_DISTURBANCE"
    | "FIRE_ANOMALY"
    | "VEGETATION_CHANGE"
    | "RADAR_CHANGE"
    | "FIELD_REPORT";

  confidenceValue?: number;
  confidenceMeaning?: string;

  spatialResolutionMeters?: number;

  forestClassification?: string;

  processingVersion: string;

  rawEvidenceRef?: string;

  qualityFlags: string[];
};
```

Maintain distinct timestamps.

An observation made on Monday but published on Thursday is not a Monday real-time alert.

Do not confuse:

- acquisition time;
- publication time;
- ingestion time;
- investigation time.

This distinction is mandatory for credible latency and intervention metrics.

# Grants and Incentives

## Status

**PLANNED research discipline.** This document does not claim eligibility for any grant or incentive.

## Funding-fit principles

Potential grant or catalytic funding can support validation of public-interest infrastructure, data-access research, local capacity building, open methods, or pilot evaluation. It should not distort product priorities or become a substitute for an institutional customer finding the workflow useful.

## Required review before pursuit

- Eligibility, geography, applicant identity, and deadline from the official issuer.
- Permitted costs, data-rights implications, reporting burden, co-funding, and intellectual-property requirements.
- Whether a proposal implies unsupported environmental, legal, or impact claims.
- Whether the activity strengthens authentic observation, defensible evidence, or outcome follow-up.

## Incentive integrity

Avoid alert-count incentives. Desired incentives reward accurate review, field-verification quality, evidence completeness, justified follow-up, and clearly bounded outcome documentation. Field participants must never be pressured to expose confidential locations or make unsupported allegations.

## Record format

For each opportunity, record issuer, official URL, eligibility, deadline, scope, cost share, data/IP terms, privacy risks, required partners, evidence basis, decision owner, and status. No outreach or submission is authorized by this document.

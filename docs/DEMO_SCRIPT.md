# Three-Minute DEMO Script

## Preconditions

Run in DEMO mode unless a verified live provider is configured. Every fixture, coordinate, evidence asset, and metric shown as synthetic must visibly display DEMO. Do not present it as a current Kenyan incident.

## Narrative

### 0:00–0:25 — The operating gap

Open the Aberdare monitoring view. Say: Satellite alerts already exist. The gap is turning observations into documented, accountable conservation action.

### 0:25–0:50 — A reviewable observation

Select the DEMO observation. Show source, observation/published/ingested timestamps, spatial resolution, quality flags, and the explicit DEMO label. State that an alert is not proof of illegal activity.

### 0:50–1:15 — Inspection context

Open the available before/after or imagery-context panel. Read the acquisition dates, source labels, resolution, and limitations. If a suitable pair does not exist, say so; do not manufacture a comparison.

### 1:15–1:40 — Legal and land context

Show documented or simulated legal references with their source status and uncertainty. Explain that any real legal conclusion requires an official source and qualified human review.

### 1:40–2:00 — Investigation case

Create or review a case. Assign an authorized reviewer, show state history, list missing evidence, and record why verification is required.

### 2:00–2:25 — Evidence and response

Attach a DEMO field record or permitted evidence asset. Show its hash/provenance, record a response, and preserve the audit history.

### 2:25–2:45 — EvidenceProof dossier

Generate the dossier. Show observation chronology, sources, uncertainty, evidence manifest, actions, and outstanding questions.

### 2:45–3:00 — Outcome graph

Show the linked observation, case, evidence, action, follow-up, and outcome record. Close: The satellite sees a change. ForestProof helps establish what happened, what was done, and what was later observed.

## Demo constraints

Do not claim enforcement, hectares saved, live satellite surveillance, court findings, or causation from a DEMO flow.

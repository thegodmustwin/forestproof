# AlphaZero-Inspired Prioritization Doctrine

## Status

**PLANNED.** The AlphaZero language is a decision-making analogy, not a reason to claim reinforcement learning, Monte Carlo tree search, or autonomous enforcement.

## P0 approach

Use a transparent, human-overridable priority heuristic. Possible visible factors:

    environmental_importance
    evidence_strength
    disturbance_magnitude
    urgency
    current_restrictions
    repeated_incident_history
    verification_feasibility
    expected_investigation_cost

Every factor requires a definition, input provenance, limitations, and a clear explanation in the review interface.

## Future evidence threshold

Only after sufficient verified, representative outcomes exist may ForestProof evaluate calibrated statistical methods, active learning, information-gain sampling, or outcome-informed prioritization. Evaluation must use geographically and temporally appropriate holdouts to avoid leakage.

## Guardrails

- A score is a prioritization aid, not an allegation or legal finding.
- Do not use secret scoring to trigger coercive external action.
- Keep historical outcomes, false positives, and uncertainty visible.
- Add adverse scenarios to regression testing: plantations, cloud shadow, seasonal change, fire, storm damage, delayed publication, duplicate products, bad coordinates, outdated documents, and conflicting field reports.

# Pilot Partnerships

## Objective

Secure one legitimate conservation or watershed organization to use ForestProof on a defined monitoring workflow and provide structured feedback. The partner retains authority over its operational decisions; ForestProof provides bounded decision support and evidence organization.

## Suitable pilot profile

- Has a lawful mandate or legitimate responsibility for a defined area or programme.
- Can nominate authorized reviewers and a workflow owner.
- Understands that remote observations require verification and may be wrong.
- Can discuss evidence, privacy, data rights, and local safety constraints.
- Can provide feedback about workflow friction and useful reports.

## Pilot scope

1. Agree a monitored area and data-source configuration.
2. Configure an evidence and review policy.
3. Run clearly labelled DEMO cases, then LIVE cases only after source activation.
4. Review observation provenance and uncertainty.
5. Create a case, assign a reviewer, attach permitted evidence, record an action, and capture a follow-up.
6. Measure review time, evidence completeness, source reliability, and user feedback.

## Partnership safeguards

- Do not promise enforcement, legal conclusions, or prevention outcomes.
- Do not contact institutions, share private data, or issue notifications without authorization.
- Set data ownership, controller/processor roles, retention, security, and export terms before handling sensitive material.
- Respect local field safety and confidential-source protection.

## Success signal

The immediate success metric is operational usefulness: a professional can understand a sourced observation and complete an accountable evidence workflow with less friction. It is not raw alert volume.

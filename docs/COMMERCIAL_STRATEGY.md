# Commercial Strategy

## Positioning

ForestProof sells operational evidence and coordination value, not a claim to own satellite observation. Its initial promise is: turn environmental disturbances into evidence-backed investigation cases, track authorized response, and document what was later observed.

## Initial customers

- Conservation organizations.
- Watershed and water-catchment managers.
- Restoration and forestry operators with legitimate monitoring duties.
- Environmental-risk, compliance, and reporting teams.
- Funders requiring stronger intervention documentation.

## Commercial wedge

The smallest paid offer is an institutional pilot for one approved monitoring area: sourced observations, reviewable cases, evidence records, action/follow-up workflow, and defensible reporting.

## What is not sold as a P0 claim

- Autonomous determination of illegal logging.
- Guaranteed prevention of forest loss.
- Exact hydrological or biodiversity impact from a single detection.
- Continuous live surveillance or globally complete coverage.
- Legal advice or enforcement services.

## Validation ladder

1. A legitimate professional examines a case and finds the workflow useful.
2. An organization agrees to a structured pilot.
3. A customer pays for monitoring, evidence management, or reporting.
4. Repeat use creates verified workflow history and measured operational improvement.

## Future revenue paths

Only after validation: monitored-area subscription, evidence/reporting plans, secure organizational seats, API/webhooks, retention tiers, and paid pilot management. Do not implement billing, enterprise integration, or pricing commitments before a real customer need is evidenced.

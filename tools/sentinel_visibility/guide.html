Learn to read an observation

First ask how much we could see.

A satellite pass can cover a place on the map while clouds, shadow or missing data leave much of the ground unassessed. The coverage report makes that gap visible.

A few useful terms

Make the technical labels familiar.

Use these meanings when reading the explorer and following the tour.

Visible
A sampled cell centre classified as usable surface in the scene-quality layer. It says the source could assess that part of the view.
Unassessed
A sampled centre affected by cloud, shadow, missing or unclassified data. Its land condition remains unknown from that pass.
Image grid
A satellite product's named tile, such as T36MZE. The two Geta grids overlap, so their counts must be read separately.
Vegetation classification
The scene-quality layer's vegetation class within usable centres. It is not a measurement of forest loss.
Provisional research area
An engineering rectangle used to evaluate sources. It is not an official Geta Forest boundary or an approved operational alert filter.
Source receipt
A retained record of the source files, dates and fingerprints used for an observation. It helps another reviewer retrace the input.
Case
A human-owned record connecting an observation, review, response and follow-up. Opening a case makes no finding about cause or legality.
Outcome lineage
The recorded connections from a source observation to case work. Environmental impact needs additional evidence beyond those workflow links.

Common questions

What does this mean in practice?

Does a change in a satellite signal prove forest loss?

It is a reason to examine the source and context. Fire, seasonal vegetation, management activity, weather, sensor effects and classification errors can produce different signals. Ground conditions and cause require further evidence.

Does an observation establish that an activity is illegal?

No legal conclusion follows from a satellite signal. Legal applicability needs authoritative source documents, the right time and location, and qualified human review. ForestProof has no verified operative court-order or permit corpus loaded.

How often is the public coverage checked?

The source-check timer is configured for 03:15 and 15:15 East Africa Time. A new check does not guarantee a new usable acquisition. Read the actual report-verification time and each observation date on the explorer. Failed or overdue checks are labelled.

Can I keep exploring if JavaScript is disabled?

Yes. The coverage report retains its full dated archive. This guide and the research explanation also work without JavaScript, including the threshold comparison.

Can I upload a real incident or personal field information?

The shared demo is for synthetic records. Real evidence uploads and public registration are not available. Private evidence, credentials and owner-review notes are kept outside the public experience.

Who is ForestProof being built for?

Curious visitors can inspect coverage and research. Enthusiasts can learn how to question an observation. The longer-term workflow is for legitimate conservation and watershed teams with a real monitoring, review or reporting responsibility. No partner endorsement or measured conservation impact is claimed.

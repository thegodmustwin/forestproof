From observation to follow-up

Example · A reviewer notices a possible change in a clear image.

  1. Check the evidence

    Save the source and observation date. Record which areas were clear and which were hidden by cloud or shadow.

  2. Record a decision

    A reviewer compares the images and records what they can establish, what remains uncertain, and who will follow up.

  3. Set a follow-up

    Schedule another observation or a field check. Link the result to the original evidence so someone else can retrace the decision.

Observe. Review. Respond.

Every observation deserves a traceable next step.

ForestProof connects source evidence, a responsible reviewer and a dated follow-up. Another person can retrace what was learned and what needs checking.

  1. Understand the signal

    Read the source, observation date and usable coverage. Record what the evidence can establish and where the view is limited.

  2. Own the investigation

    A reviewer opens a case, preserves the evidence, and records a decision and an authorized response.

  3. Return to the evidence

    Schedule the next check. Document what happened and connect the follow-up to the original observation.

The protected case application demonstrates this workflow with labelled synthetic records. Take the product tour
